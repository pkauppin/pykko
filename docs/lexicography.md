## Compounds

In determining whether a word is monolithic or a compound, the following rules are applied in the following order.

1. Any word that the _Kielitoimiston sanakirja_ does not treat as a compound *is not a compound*.
   - E.g. _maailma_, _mikroskooppi_, _sisilisko_, _papukaija_, _hehtaari_ are not compounds.
   - These analyses are based on either their etymology (_sisilisko_, _papukaija_) or their prosody (_maailma_).
1. Unadapted borrowings from foreign languages (a.k.a. _sitaattilainat_) are never considered compounds.
   - These are (recent) borrowings that do not conform to Finnish spelling, pronunciation, or phonotactics.
   - E.g. _agar-agar_, _beatboxing_, _backstage_, _offset_, _freelancer_, _babysitter_, _breakdance_, _pannacotta_ etc. are not compounds.
1. So called "obvious" compounds:
   1. Words that are diachronically Finnish/Finnic compounds and are stilla analyzable as such are compounds.
      - E.g. _leikki|puisto_
   1. Words that can be meaningfully (both formally and semantically) analyzed as consisting of two or more words are _synchronically_ compounds.
      - E.g. _napa|kaira_
   1. Words that are not learned borrowings and have been suffixed with an undisputably native Finnish word are compounds.
      - E.g. _baby|pinaatti_ is a type of _pinaatti_ and ends with the word _pinaatti_ => compound.
1. Units of measurement prefixed with the SI prefixes _mikro-_, _milli-_, _kilo-_, _hehto-_, _mega-_, _giga-_, _nano-_ etc. are compounds.
   - E.g. _kilo|metri_, _kilo|tavu_, _giga|tonni_ are all compounds. In cases like _kilometri_, the analysis as a compund is supported by its front vowel harmony.
   - **NB!**: _hehtaari_ is not a compound.
   - **NB!**: _mikro-_ is both an SI prefix as well as a more general Greek prefix meaning ”very small" or "pertaining to very small things". Not all instances where the latter are compounds. See below.
1.Words like _mikro|ilme_, _bio|hajoava_, _bio|muovi_, _geo|kätkö_, and _nano|kone_ means that the foreign learned prefixes  _mikro-_, _bio-_, _geo-_, and _nano-_ are used productively in compounding with native words.
   - This entails that _mikro|biologia_, _bio|teknologia_, _nano|teknologia_, and _geo|politiikka_ can be synchnocally treated as compounds, despite being learned borrowings.
1. Word containing a learned prefix but not ending with a words that appears in Finnish as independent word are not compounds.
   - _mikroskopia_, _geotooppi_ (*_skopia_ and *_tooppi_ are not independent words in Finnish)
   - Cases of learned prefix followed by _-skooppi_ are not compounds (see _mikroskooppi_).
1. If vowel harmony work over where a compound boundary could or should be, the word is not a compound:
   - E.g. _hydrometri_ and _mikrometri_ (the tool, not the unit) take both front and back harmony, and as such are not compounds (*_hydro|metri_)

## Interjections

Any noun can be used as a swear, and as such, the list of interjections does not include words like _helvetti_, _paska_, _vittu_ etc. These are always treated as nouns.
Same goes for terms of address.
**NB!** The word "kiitos" is not a swear and is therefore included, despite also being a noun.
